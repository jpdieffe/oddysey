New mythic bosses
=================

The Cerberus, Medusa, Talos, Typhon, Siren Queen, and Hecatoncheires sprite
foundations were created specifically for Odyssey with OpenAI image generation,
informed by open-game sprite research and normalized into a fixed 3x2 sheet.
